Forge build (prompts.json): seed 43, motion_seed 0, motion_retries 1, describe "a tall swift
lithe sky warrior, pale gold crested helm and chest plate, verdigris trim, bare sinewy arms,
thick separated legs, fitted dark shorts, compact gold greaves". This was the FIFTH attempt at
the concept: the four earlier describes (the Stage 3 spec's "sentinel lancer ... tall crested
helm ... fitted greaves" and its variants) hid the knees or the elbows under plate and the rig
stage could not fit them; "bare sinewy arms, thick separated legs, fitted dark shorts" is what
finally gave the fitter both knees, both elbows and both ankles (rig-report: status ready,
fitted knee 2 / elbow 2 / ankle 2, 23,995 tris). No wings, cape or plume were asked for: the
light-wings are Unity VFX (SeraphLancerVisuals), the javelin is a Unity projectile mesh.
Jab2 / ShoulderCharge / HeavyAttack / ComboFinisher are the shared kit's own specs
(motion_sources.json, generated at the kit's seeds 1010 / 4 / 1014 / 1016); JavelinThrow "a
person throws overhand with their right arm" (@1.2 #auto ~1.4, contact 0.696, right wrist,
anticipation 0.464 s: the arm-back IS the tell); HoverHold "a person stands still with both
arms held out to the sides" (@0.8 ~1, no contact, a held aerial pose: the frozen-pose failure
used on purpose). No per-clip GIF previews were written into this export folder (the build ran
without --preview), so the clips were judged from the manifest beats and the Blender probe, not
from a filmstrip; the lead's 4b log and the Sandbox smoke are the first look at them moving.
